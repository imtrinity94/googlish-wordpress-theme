Makes the Googlish theme yours without editing the theme.

== What it does ==

Replaces four Googlish theme sections with your own content:

* Header: wordmark, navigation links, search placeholder
* Footer: links, social icons, copyright line
* Home page author section: photo, name, role, bio, blog stats, social links, badges
* Home page tools section: title, line, button and cards

It also sets your X handle for "Share to X" links.

== Editing your details ==

Everything lives in data/profile.json. Put photos in assets/ (refer to them as "assets/file.jpg") or use full URLs.
After editing, upload the plugin again (Plugins > Add New > Upload > Replace current with uploaded).

== Requirements ==

The Googlish theme, version 2.9.2 or later.
